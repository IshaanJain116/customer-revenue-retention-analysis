# 📊 Customer Revenue & Retention Analysis

An end-to-end Customer Analytics project built using **Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI** to analyze customer purchasing behavior, revenue contribution, subscription impact, repeat purchase trends, and customer retention patterns.

---

# 🚀 Project Highlights

✔ Performed detailed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using Python  
✔ Solved real-world customer business problems using SQL  
✔ Built an interactive Power BI dashboard for revenue & retention insights  
✔ Analyzed customer spending behavior and purchase trends  
✔ Identified high-value and repeat customer segments  
✔ Created business insights for customer retention strategies  
✔ Developed dynamic KPIs and interactive visualizations  

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior  
- Study customer retention and repeat purchase patterns  
- Identify high-revenue customer segments  
- Compare subscription vs non-subscription customer behavior  
- Understand discount dependency across products  
- Analyze product category revenue contribution  
- Generate actionable business insights for decision-making

# 📌 Key Insights

- Middle-aged and adult customers contributed the highest overall revenue.  
- Subscribed customers generated higher total revenue compared to non-subscribers.  
- Clothing and accessories categories dominated total sales contribution.  
- Repeat buyers showed stronger customer retention behavior.  
- Some products demonstrated higher dependency on discounts for sales generation.  
- Customer satisfaction ratings remained relatively stable across major product categories.
