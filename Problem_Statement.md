# Problem Statement

In today’s competitive retail and e-commerce environment, understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving revenue generation, customer retention, and business decision-making. Companies collect large amounts of customer transaction data, but without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify meaningful business insights and customer trends.

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior data to uncover valuable insights related to customer spending patterns, subscription impact, repeat purchase behavior, product performance, and revenue contribution across different customer segments.

The objective of this project is to perform an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to transform raw customer transaction data into actionable business insights. The analysis aims to answer important business questions such as:

- Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue?
- Are subscribed customers generating more revenue than non-subscribers?
- Which products receive the highest customer satisfaction ratings?
- Which product categories contribute most to total sales?
- How do discounts influence purchasing behavior?
- Which age groups and customer segments show strong retention behavior?
- What shipping methods are associated with higher purchase amounts?

The project includes:
- Data cleaning and preprocessing using Python
- Feature engineering and customer segmentation
- Business-focused SQL analysis using PostgreSQL
- Interactive dashboard creation using Power BI
- Revenue and retention trend visualization

The final outcome of this project helps simulate how businesses can use data analytics to improve customer understanding, optimize revenue strategies, and support data-driven decision-making.
